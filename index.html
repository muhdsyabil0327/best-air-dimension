Company Background & Decades of Technical Mastery

Founded in 2015 and operating as a dynamic partnership since 2020, BEST AIR DIMENSION TECHNOLOGY AND SERVICES is a premier Bumiputera-owned engineering contractor based in Gelugor, Pulau Pinang[cite: 1]. Fully registered with CIDB (Grade G1), MOF, and PKK, we specialize in Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning (HVAC), electrical engineering, fire protection, and civil maintenance solutions across Northern Malaysia.

At the helm of our operations is our Founder and Manager, Badrul Husam bin Abdullah, working in close partnership with Assistant Manager Muhammad Nabil Aiman bin Badrul Husam. Bringing over 20 years of hands-on industry experience in HVAC and Mechanical & Electrical (M&E) engineering, the management provides strategic direction and certified technical leadership that drive our success. With regulatory credentials including official Chargeman A0 licensing from the Energy Commission (Suruhanjaya Tenaga) and Gas Management Certifications from the Department of Environment (Jabatan Alam Sekitar), the company consistently delivers high-standard maintenance and installation projects for major public institutions such as JKR Pulau Pinang, USM, and MBPP.
